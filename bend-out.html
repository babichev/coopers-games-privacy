Privacy Policy — Bend Out

Last updated: 28 October 2026

About this notice

This notice covers the closed-testing edition of Bend Out for Android (com.coopersgames.bendout) and support or privacy messages about the game. The publisher is Maksim Babichev, operating as Coopers Games, based in Cyprus. Privacy contact: maxim@babichev.me.

Game data on your device

The game saves puzzle and campaign progress, the current attempt, coins, lives, boosters, tutorial and reward receipts, preferences and privacy choices on your device. It also keeps local gameplay and technical records used to run the game, restore its state and investigate errors. These can include local session and attempt identifiers, saved progress metadata, timestamps, puzzle identifiers and event details.

A previous save or a copy of a damaged save may be retained for recovery. Local diagnostic journals rotate according to size; this is not a fixed number of days. Progress, preferences, privacy-choice records, technical ledgers and recovery files do not have a general automatic expiry.

Optional services and online features

Remote Analytics, Crash reports and Attribution are unavailable in this edition. The app contains Firebase and AppsFlyer components configured with these features disabled. Local diagnostics can still be written while remote measurement is unavailable. This notice does not describe an edition with active measurement services.

The game does not offer player accounts, player-to-player communication, app-operated cloud saves, advertising or real-money purchases. Its virtual rewards cannot be exchanged for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or other real-world value. We do not sell game records or use them for behavioural advertising.

Resetting and deleting local data

Reset progress resets only part of the game. It does not erase every record, preference, diagnostic journal or backup. Changing a privacy choice is also not a request to erase existing local files.

To remove the game's local data from a device, use the clear-storage or clear-app-data option in Android's settings for Bend Out. This removes local progress and settings and cannot be undone through the game.

Android or your device provider may back up, transfer or restore app data according to the device, software and backup settings. Uninstalling the game does not necessarily remove copies held by those services. Manage those copies through the relevant device or backup settings. We do not operate the device's backup service.

Local game files are not encrypted by the game itself. Their protection also depends on your device and Android settings. An integrity check or encoded value is not encryption.

If you contact us

If you email maxim@babichev.me, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachment you choose to send. Please send only what is needed to explain the issue; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

We use correspondence to answer your request, provide necessary follow-up and meet applicable legal obligations. Where the GDPR applies, our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry is the basis for ordinary support correspondence; processing required by law relies on that obligation. We keep correspondence while needed for those purposes, including necessary resolution or legal records, rather than retaining it indefinitely without a purpose.

Our support email uses Google's email services. Your email provider and our provider process information needed for delivery, storage and security under their applicable arrangements. That processing may occur outside Cyprus or your country. Google's privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google's published information about international transfers and safeguards is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks. Access to correspondence is limited to those who need it to handle the request or provide the relevant service.

Your choices and rights

The intended audience is ages 13 and over. If a child has sent personal information to us, a parent or guardian can contact the privacy address.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, object to relevant processing and complain to a competent data-protection authority. Contact maxim@babichev.me. We may need proportionate information to locate the correspondence and verify a request. We do not operate a player-account deletion service because the game has no player accounts; clearing local game storage is a separate action.

Changes

If the game's data practices change, we will update this notice and provide any notice or choice required before starting the changed processing. The date above identifies this version.
